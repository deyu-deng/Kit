常见问题

When is escaping actually required?
Always escape < and & when they appear as text — < starts a tag the parser will try to execute, and & starts an entity the parser will try to resolve. Quotes matter inside attribute values.

Why does © render literally sometimes?
A missing semicolon. Browsers repair some entity typos, but only named entities with semicolons are guaranteed — write &copy; not &copy.

Entity vs numeric reference?
Named (&copy;) is readable; numeric (&#169;) covers every Unicode character, including ones with no named entity. Both are ASCII-safe for emails and old parsers.
