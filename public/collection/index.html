Quality Website Collection

The internet has millions of tool sites; maybe a hundred deserve a permanent bookmark. This collection is our hand-maintained shortlist: websites we actually use, each with an honest description of what it does, why it is good, and who it is for. No SEO filler, no affiliate rankings — if an entry stops being good, it gets removed.

What gets in

  • Works in the browser — no install required for the core use case.
  • Genuinely useful for free — the free tier must stand on its own.
  • Respectable privacy posture — no dark patterns, no data resale.
  • Actually maintained — abandoned projects do not survive a re-review.
