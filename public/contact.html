About the Repository Name

You may notice our code lives under the NoCloud project on GitHub. "NoCloud" is the engineering codename for our local-first philosophy — the same principle that powers Plobi-kit: your data stays on your device, not in someone's cloud. Plobi-kit is the public-facing brand; NoCloud is the architecture it is built on.

Response Time
