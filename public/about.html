About Plobi-kit

Welcome to Plobi-kit, a clean, high-performance portal of online utility applications that streamlines everyday work in the browser — for developers, designers, students, and anyone who needs a tool that just works.

Our Mission

We believe online tools should be simple, beautiful, and blazing fast. Too many tools online are cluttered with heavy layouts, intrusive popups, slow server round-trips, and mandatory account registrations just to paste a JSON string. Plobi-kit is built on a "Vanilla First, Local First" principle: every single tool in our collection runs entirely inside your browser, with zero server uploads, zero data collection, and zero login requirements. Your work stays on your device — always.

Plobi-kit started as an open-source project to give the web community a single, unified, ad-supported toolbox that remains free forever. We are a small, independent team of web developers and designers who believe that productivity tools shouldn't cost money, require subscriptions, or compromise your privacy to be useful.

Why Plobi-kit Is Different

  • 100% Client-Side Processing: All computations — QR generation, regex matching, image compression, JWT decoding, SVG optimization — occur directly in your browser's JavaScript engine. None of your sensitive credentials, source code, or private files are ever sent to a server.
  • No Account Required: Use every tool immediately without creating an account, providing an email address, or agreeing to a paywall. Developer tools should be instantly accessible.
  • Offline-Capable PWA: Plobi-kit is built as a Progressive Web App. Once loaded, many tools work without an internet connection — ideal for use during flights, on trains, or in network-restricted corporate environments.
  • Minimal, Focused Design: Inspired by the design systems of Vercel and Linear, our clean layout provides precise typography, consistent spacing, and clear visual hierarchy — a distraction-free environment where your work is front and center.
  • Bilingual Support: Every tool and interface element is available in both English and Simplified Chinese, making Plobi-kit equally accessible to users across the globe.

Meet the Team

  • David Deng — Founder & Lead Developer. Full-stack engineer focused on privacy-respecting, local-first web tooling. GitHub

We keep the team deliberately small so that every tool carries the same guarantee: your input stays on your device — no accounts, and no collection of anything you paste or compress. The only server actions are the ones you trigger yourself, like share links and the contact form. Our code is published openly under the NoCloud project — "NoCloud" is our local-first engineering codename, and Plobi-kit is the public brand built on top of it.
