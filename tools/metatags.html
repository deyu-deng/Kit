Tool Documentation & Usage Guide

Meta tags are snippets of text that describe a page's content; the meta tags don't appear on the page itself, but only in the page's source code.

Why use social media previews?

Open Graph (for Facebook and LinkedIn) and Twitter Cards let you control what image, title, and descriptive summaries are shown when your page link is posted on social feeds. Rich cards significantly increase click-through rates.

Robots Directives

The robots tag instructs search engine crawlers (like Googlebot) whether they are allowed to index the page (index) and follow the links on it (follow).
