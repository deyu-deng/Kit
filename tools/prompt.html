Tool Documentation & Usage Guide

Prompt engineering is the process of structuring a text query so that it can be interpreted and understood by a generative AI model.

Why use structured templates?

Simple queries like "Review this code" often yield generic or superficial advice from LLMs. By assigning a specific persona, defining clear review constraints, and structuring formatting rules, you force the AI to return detailed, high-fidelity responses.

Security and Offline Privacy

Our prompt helper runs 100% locally in your browser. No prompt content is ever cached or sent to external servers, protecting intellectual property or proprietary code.
